  unchanged: `layout/logic_tile.def` and `layout/logic_tile.gds` are

  `flow/tool_versions.sh`. The layout lineage's `RECORDED_KLT_VERSION` is
  **not** moved: `flow/layout.sh` check mode under 0.8.0 does not reproduce the
  committed GDS or routed DEF, so the layout artifacts were not regenerated.

## Method

`./flow/sta-sweep.sh` (check mode) first, which failed on exactly the
regenerated reports listed below; then `./flow/sta-sweep.sh --update`. Same
three single-corner runs per corner and the same one multi-corner request as the
superseded record; `flow/sta_envelope_check.py` passed (18/18 ratified corners,
all constrained, setup/hold non-negative, SPEF annotation complete, binding
corner `ss_n40C_1v28` WNS 15.2145 ns, cross-checked against the per-corner
reports).

## Result: nothing moved except metadata

Every `*.sta.json` under `measurements/timing-characterization/` (36
per-corner reports and the envelope) was compared key by key against the
superseded copy. Differences are exactly:

- `schema_version` 1 -> 2 on every per-corner report;
- `spef_sha256` `5101e831...` -> `5a278e04...` on every SPEF run. The SPEF
  diff is the single `*VERSION` header line (`0.7.0+g6fd0278268cc` ->
  `0.8.0`);
- new fields the 0.8.0 `klt sta` response adds (`clock_mode`, and per corner
  `clock_propagation`, `constraint_completeness`, `electrical_checks`,
  `power_basis`, `worst_setup_path`, `worst_hold_path`, `sdf`, deck paths, and
  extra `spef_annotation` detail).

All WNS, hold slack, TNS, violation counts, `fmax_mhz` and `timing_status`
values are identical at all 18 corners, LEF-only and SPEF-annotated. The
figure of record (SPEF WNS **15.2145 ns** at `ss_n40C_1v28`) is unchanged.

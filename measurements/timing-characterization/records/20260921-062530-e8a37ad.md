## Why the committed SDF is the 2026-09-18 lineage, not a fresh re-write

`flow/sdf-resim.sh`'s trust chain refuses to overwrite the committed SDF
unless the regenerating run's own `spef_sta.annotation_complete` is
true. On the 2026-09-21 verifying host, the operator-local `openroad`
wrapper resolves to image `26Q3-2276-g4a7cf9b22a`, under whose OpenSTA
build the post-route SPEF annotation of this design's
escaped-identifier nets regresses to incomplete (the same
klayout-tools#1623/#1624 family resurfacing through a moved tool), so
the harness correctly refuses to update. The committed SDF therefore
remains the 2026-09-18 recorded-toolchain artifact -- whose producing
lineage passes the harness's own byte-identical DEF gate (`layout/
logic_tile.def` regenerates byte-identical under the installed klt; see
the STA successor record `20260921-062500-e8a37ad`'s "Re-verification"
for the full picture). Nothing about this record or the harness treats
the block as anything other than what it is: a publish-history fact
with a reproduced, cited upstream cause.
